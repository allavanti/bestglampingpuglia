In focus · Selected places

Five of the most interesting places in Puglia, told one by one: what makes them special, what to know before you go, who they suit.

Bisceglie · BATOur pick · Couples · Families

Wishne Agricamp

Spacious tents in an olive grove near Bisceglie and Trani, among hammocks, trulli and silence, with two hosts who make you feel at home and a shared table in the evening. Our favourite place to sleep outdoors in Puglia. Good to know: compost bathroom.

Read the profile →
Mattinata · FGGargano · Sea

Le Lenze Don Mimì

At Mattinatella on the Gargano, among olive trees and a few steps from the beach. Pool, restaurant and attentive staff; our tip is to pick a big tent.

Read the profile →
Vignanotica · FGGargano · Sea

HMO Glamping & Suites

Tents that feel like suites: a private garden, a tub and the bay of Vignanotica five minutes on foot. Allow for the climb back in the heat, isolation and a car.

Read the profile →
Galatone · LESalento · Farm stay

Tenuta DonnAnna

A farm stay with tents at Galatone, in the Salento hinterland. Home-style breakfast, a pool always available and family hospitality. The sea is about a quarter of an hour by car.

Read the profile →
Alliste · LEIonian Salento · Camping

Macaria Camp

Roomy tents among olive trees, at Capilungo, about 800 metres from the Ionian sea. A hammock and a gazebo for each, a shared kitchen and Rocco and Giovanni to welcome you; allow for shared facilities and little shade.
