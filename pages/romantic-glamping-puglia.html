Romantic glamping in Puglia: quiet, privacy, landscape

Two very different places, a canvas suite on the Gargano and an olive grove among friends, and how to choose.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Back to home

A stay for two works when there is quiet, a space that is only yours and a place that moves attention away from the everyday. Among the five we have profiled, two are the ones we would think of first, and they are very different: one is a soft luxury with canvas in place of walls, the other a field of olive trees where you feel like a guest at a friend’s house.

Soft luxury: HMO at Vignanotica

HMO is the closest to a suite. The tent is spacious, with wood, rugs, air conditioning and a private garden among the olive trees where the aperitif is taken with the sea at the end of the view; in some there is a tub you leave reluctantly, in others an outdoor hot tub, wood-fired. In the evening you dine in the olive grove, in the morning there is a full breakfast, and the bay of Vignanotica is five minutes on foot. The phone does not get a signal: for two people who want to switch off, that is a virtue.

Worth knowing: the price is that of a suite, and the climb back from the beach in the hot hours is best done by golf cart. In summer, at weekends, the bay is busy with boats.

Simplicity: Wishne near Bisceglie

Wishne is the opposite, and that is why it works: an olive grove in the Lama Santa Croce park, a few tents, hammocks, trulli, and two hosts who dine with their guests and make you feel at home. There is nothing sophisticated: a comfortable tent in a beautiful place, a long evening with grilled meat and a glass of wine. The bathroom is next to the tent, private but of the compost type: anyone looking for luxury will find it basic, anyone looking for contact with the land will appreciate it.

A basic tent in an extraordinary position is worth more than a lodge overloaded with decoration in an anonymous place.

Two other ways to stay as a couple

On the Gargano, Le Lenze Don Mimì combines olive grove, pebble bay, pool and restaurant, and our advice is to choose a big tent. In the Salento, DonnAnna offers a family welcome and a breakfast you remember; less intimacy, more warmth.

What makes the difference

Few units, so you do not hear the others coming and going. A bathroom of your own or at least a private one. A place where the olive grove or the sea is within a step. And someone to welcome you: in the places that work there is almost always a family living there. For the full picture of the five places there is the general guide.
