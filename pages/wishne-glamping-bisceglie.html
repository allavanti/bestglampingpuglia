In focus · Wishne Agricamp, Bisceglie (BT), Puglia

Wishne Glamping near Bisceglie: a night among the olive trees

In the Lama Santa Croce park, near Trani and Bisceglie. Roomy tents, centuries-old olive trees, and two people waiting for you.

WhereCountryside near Bisceglie (BT), Lama Santa Croce park
What it isAn agri-camp with tents in an olive grove
The seaBisceglie, Blue Flag, 10 minutes away
Your hostsNicola and Lila — and Coco, the dog
Good to knowHot afternoons in high summer; compost-type bathroom

You drive into the countryside, and at some point the noise of the world switches off. At Wishne a dog, Coco, comes to meet you, then Nicola and Lila, who run the place and welcome you the way you welcome a friend you haven’t seen in a while. The rest — the olive trees, the tents, the silence — follows by itself.

Among the olive trees, with no neighbours at your elbow

The estate is big, and you feel it. Centuries-old olive trees, fruit trees, a few well-kept trulli, and tents scattered far enough apart that everyone has their own piece of countryside and their own peace. In the morning the view opens across the Puglian landscape, and breakfast is taken looking at it, unhurried, coffee in hand and nothing to do.

Night is something else. The silence is the kind only the countryside can give, and even in August, after days that touch forty degrees, the air cools enough that a blanket is welcome. You sleep with the stars above the tent, and you wake up remembering it.

A tent where you are truly comfortable

The tents are spacious, in a relaxed, ethnic-inspired style, already pitched and ready: perfect if you want a night in the countryside without the effort of camping. Comfortable beds, everything spotless, the car parked a few steps away. Each tent has its own bathroom, next to the tent but enclosed and private, with a shower, of the compost type: if you like eco-friendly solutions you will appreciate it, and if you have never tried one it is better to know before you arrive. Outside, hammocks and small tables where an aperitivo is taken in the shade, when the sun is still high and you already feel on holiday.

Nicola, Lila and a shared table

But Wishne is above all about people. Nicola and Lila bring a passion you can feel from the first minute: they tell you about their land, walk you around the estate, let you pick figs and mulberries straight from the tree, and see you off with a bag of freshly picked prickly pears. In autumn you can go and pick olives together. If you arrive late, say after a delayed ferry, someone is waiting for you with a glass of cold water. Nobody obliges you to anything: the olive oil, the trees, the hidden caves around here are suggestions, not programmes, and you take them up if you feel like it.

In the evening, if you wish, everyone sits down together. A dinner prepared by Nicola, at a table with the other guests, with grilled meat and a glass of wine, and vegetarian for anyone who asks. You eat, you talk, you tell each other where you come from: guests at Wishne arrive from all over Europe, and more than one says the evening is what stayed with them. It is not a restaurant, so it is best to arrange it in advance.

Why Wishne stands out among Puglia’s glamping

Plenty of places in Puglia call themselves glamping; many are campsites with a few more cushions. Wishne is different in ways you can feel rather than read on a brochure.

First, the hosts. At Wishne the people who run the place are the people who welcome you, who sit at the table with you and who know which cove is quiet and which cave is worth the walk. That is rare, and it is what guests remember.

Second, the space. It is not every day you find yourself among centuries-old olive trees with this much room around you. The tents are far enough apart to give you privacy without feeling isolated, and the whole estate is yours to wander — fruit trees, trulli, hammocks, bicycles, a barbecue, a shared kitchen.

Third, the freedom. A small pool, a hot tub, archery, a children’s play area, pets welcome: Wishne works for couples, families, friends travelling together, solo travellers and people who never leave home without their dog. That is not something every place in Puglia can say.

And finally, the position. You are on the northern Adriatic coast, a quieter stretch than the better-known Valle d’Itria, with Trani’s cathedral and harbour a few minutes away, Bisceglie’s old town close by, and Bari airport about 52 km. You can go back to the olive grove each evening instead of fighting for parking in a crowded resort.

Around Wishne: Trani, Bisceglie, the sea

A few minutes away is Trani, and the first time you stand still in front of the cathedral: Romanesque, dated 1143, built right on the waterfront, its pale stone lighting up against the blue at sunset. The harbour has tables on the water, and the old town is best explored on foot, among alleys and palazzi, past the Scolanova Synagogue, one of the oldest in Europe. Spring and autumn are best, with fewer people. If you prefer not to drive, a train runs from Bisceglie to Trani and Bari.

Bisceglie is more intimate: medieval alleys running down to the old harbour, the Concattedrale di San Pietro Apostolo, the Swabian-Angevin Castle. In the surrounding countryside there are Bronze Age dolmens, such as the Chianca.

The sea here is made of pebbles and rocks, not sand: water shoes are useful, and anyone dreaming of sand should head towards Trani. Salsello, with its white pebbles, is lovely if you arrive early in summer; La Torretta, on the border between the two towns, is quieter.

A base camp in the heart of Puglia

The sea at Bisceglie, awarded the Blue Flag, is ten minutes away: close enough for a swim after breakfast and a return to the olive trees in the hottest hours, without having to plan an expedition.

Then there is the position. Wishne sits almost halfway across Puglia, which makes it an ideal base camp: in a day you can climb onto the Murgia, among masserie, woods and stone villages; head down to Bari and along the Bari coast, with its harbours and seaside towns; or reach Matera, with its Sassi lighting up at dusk. You leave in the morning and come back at night to a place that feels like home, without changing lodgings every second night.

Those travelling in company can also book the entire camp, for up to 22 people: a group of friends, an extended family, a small event, with the olive grove all to yourselves. It is best to arrange it in good time with Nicola and Lila.

Two things to bear in mind

In summer the daytime heat is noticeable, but the estate has plenty of shade and the nights make up for it: if heat bothers you, ask the hosts a couple of questions before you book. This is the countryside: a bit of earth, the odd insect and comfortable shoes are part of the experience. Beyond that, on site there are a hot tub, a small, cool pool, bicycles, archery, a barbecue, a shared kitchen, a play area and a play tent for children, and pets are welcome: the area is dog-proof, and dogs can run free. Small children get that little extra attention. Bari-Palese airport is about 52 km away.

Who it is for

For anyone who needs to step out of the chaos and find a little silence inside themselves: couples, families with children, groups of friends (up to 22 people if you book the whole camp), solo travellers, people who never travel without their dog. Less so for anyone looking for entertainment, nightlife or an all-inclusive package.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Wishne glamping?

Wishne Agricamp is in the countryside near Bisceglie (BT), in the Lama Santa Croce park on the northern Adriatic coast of Puglia, a few minutes from Trani. Bari-Palese airport is about 52 km away.

What is it like to stay at Wishne?

Guests sleep in spacious tents scattered through an estate of centuries-old olive trees and fruit trees, each with a private (compost) bathroom, comfortable beds, hammocks and a table outside. The hosts, Nicola and Lila, welcome guests personally and can prepare a shared dinner on request.

Is Wishne suitable for families, couples and dog owners?

Yes. There is a children’s play area, a small pool, bicycles, a hot tub, a barbecue and a shared kitchen, and pets are welcome. It suits couples, families, groups of friends and solo travellers; it is less suited to anyone looking for entertainment, nightlife or an all-inclusive resort.

Is Wishne a good base for exploring Puglia?

Yes. Wishne sits roughly in the middle of the region, so the Murgia countryside, Bari and its coast, and Matera can all be reached as day trips, and the Blue Flag sea at Bisceglie is about ten minutes away.

Can I book the whole camp for a group?

Yes. The entire property can be reserved for small groups of up to 22 people. It is best to arrange it in advance with the hosts, Nicola and Lila.

Can I get to Trani and Bisceglie without a car?

Bisceglie has a train line to Trani and Bari, so day trips are possible without driving. Trani is a few minutes from the estate by car.

Close your eyes and imagine: the silence of centuries-old olive trees, a night that is cool even in August, breakfast in front of a countryside waking up, a dog running to meet you and two people who already have a place at the table for you. That is Wishne: a way of sleeping inside Puglia and waking up as if at home.
