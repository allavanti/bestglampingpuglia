In focus · Macaria Camp, Capilungo di Alliste (LE), Salento

Macaria Camp: tents among ancient olive trees, the sea 800 metres away

A camp in the olive groves of the Ionian Salento, with real tents, a hammock and a gazebo for each. Chosen for the silence, for Rocco and Giovanni, and for a sea 800 metres away.

Good to knowShared facilities, little shade

Red earth, dry-stone walls, myrtle and capers sprouting between the rocks. Macaria Camp stands in an olive grove on the Ionian side of the Salento, and the tents are scattered beneath trees that have seen more centuries pass than it is worth counting.

Rocco, Giovanni and a camp that feels like family

You arrive in the countryside, and the person receiving guests is Rocco, with Giovanni and the family. The welcome is that of people who know the place by heart and love showing it: tips on the sea, the roads, the trips to make, and a readiness to help with any request. Some guests arrive with doubts about sleeping in a tent and leave wanting to come back.

A tent, and a corner all your own

The tents are large, set on wooden platforms, with a real bed, power sockets, a mini-fridge and windows that let the air in. They stand well apart, and in front of each one is an outdoor space with a hammock and a gazebo with sofas: the corner for staying cool in the warm hours and, in the evening, for looking up.

There is no entertainment and no crowd. The sound is the wind in the olive trees, birds in the morning, and at night a sky of stars that city dwellers have forgotten. Children feel at home too: between the tents there is room to play.

Shared kitchen, and the pleasure of a chat

The camp has a well-equipped shared kitchen, with a fridge, crockery and a washing machine, and facilities kept with care. You cook and wash beside whoever sleeps in the next tent, and that is where conversations, swapped tips and, more than once, friendships begin. Breakfast tastes of home, and a refreshment tent serves food made with local produce.

Good to know. Kitchen, bathrooms and washing machine are shared, and when the camp is full everyone waits their turn. Shade is scarce in the middle of the day: a hat and a beach umbrella help, and the best days start early.

The sea 800 metres away, and the Salento around it

The Ionian coast is a walk away, about 800 metres. At Torre San Giovanni there is white sand and clear water, and anyone who loves diving from the rocks will find more than one spot nearby. Towards Santa Maria di Leuca runs a string of sandy beaches, and among the best loved is Marina di Pescoluse, with shallow water that earned it the nickname “Maldives of the Salento”.

By car, Gallipoli is about twenty minutes away, with its old town on an island ringed by Spanish walls; Santa Maria di Leuca about forty; Lecce and Otranto a little under an hour. It is a good base for exploring from Taranto to Leuca and returning at night to a place where time slows down. Nearby are the Punta Pizzo Regional Park and the Ugento coast, with dunes and wetlands, and villages such as Felline and Ugento, which come alive with festivals in summer.

Cyclists will find country lanes for long rides, and some stop for just one night on a journey by bike. Pets are welcome: for anyone travelling with a dog, it is a place to feel at ease.

Who we recommend it to

Anyone looking for a simple, rural Ionian Salento, close to the sea and far from the crush of beach clubs. Couples, families with children, cyclists, people travelling with a dog, and anyone who loves camping but wants a real bed. Less so for anyone who wants a private bathroom, air conditioning and the services of a resort.

In the evening, with the hammock swaying gently and the wind passing through the olive trees, the Salento of crowded beaches seems very far away.
