In focus · Le Lenze Don Mimì, Mattinata (FG), Gargano

Le Lenze Don Mimì: an olive grove facing the bay

Contrada Mattinatella, on the Gargano. Well-kept tents, a pool, a restaurant and a flight of steps down to the sea.

Back to home
WhereContrada Mattinatella, Mattinata (FG), Gargano
What it isGlamping among olive trees, steps from the beach
FacilitiesPool, veranda hot tub, restaurant, playground, yoga
Good to knowYou need a car; pebble beach, five minutes on foot

At Mattinatella the olive grove runs to the edge of a plateau that looks out over the sea. Le Lenze Don Mimì has put tents there, a pool, a restaurant and just enough else that you stop wanting to be anywhere other than here.

An olive grove facing the bay

You arrive by the road, leave the car under the trees, and a golf cart takes your bags to the tent. The glamping site opened only a few years ago and sits on a plateau among olive trees, blending well into the landscape: you see it even better looking back from the sea. Mornings begin with cicadas, sometimes a rooster; evenings with wind in the leaves. After 10:30 pm quiet is the rule, and there is night-time security.

Inside the tent

The tents are new, well spaced, with a bathroom, air conditioning and a covered patio. The larger ones add a kitchen and, on the veranda, a hot tub facing the sea: it is the detail many remember first. The smaller ones, like the junior suite, are fine for a weekend and less so for a week. Inside you notice the care: a walk-in wardrobe, comfortable beds, a welcome gift of the family’s own olive oil.

Miriam, her parents and a family welcome

Behind Le Lenze are Miriam and her parents, and you can feel it. They greet you by name, tell you about the bay, and if it is your first time in glamping there is someone to walk you through it. If something is not right it is fixed fast, often before you have asked.

The table, the pool and days without a plan

The restaurant is the heart of the place: zero-mile produce, a short menu, and dinners on the terrace overlooking the gulf that stay with you. Breakfast is Italian-style, sweet, with the sea in front of you. The pool is compact, shaded by olive trees, perfect for a dip and for children, less so for swimming laps.

Around it there is whatever you need not to go anywhere: sunbeds and hammocks, a fitness area, bikes, a ping-pong table, table football, a yoga hour, massages and a playground for children (check which services are running when you book). Pets are welcome.

The sea: a flight of steps and a pebble bay

In under five minutes on foot, a private flight of steps leads down to the bay of Mattinatella. It is a pebble beach, so bring water shoes, and the water is turquoise. Umbrellas and sunbeds are provided to take down for the free section, which fills up at weekends. On site you can arrange kayaks, SUPs and boat trips, with the caves along the coast within paddling distance.

Things to bear in mind

In July and August, in the middle of the day, the tent heats up even with air conditioning: those are hours for the pool or the sea, and June and September are gentler months. The walls are canvas, so as anywhere with tents a few sounds carry, especially early in the morning. If you pick a tent with a kitchen, bring a corkscrew, a chopping board and a tea towel. For shopping, trips out and restaurants further afield you need a car.

Nearby

The Baia delle Zagare, with its two sea stacks, is the iconic bay of this stretch of the Gargano, and from the property you reach it by car. In high season access to the free beach may require a permit, with a limited number of daily entries, and parking near the bay is limited and often paid: the rules change every year, so check beforehand. Anyone who prefers to walk has a path from Mattinatella, of medium difficulty and about 90 minutes each way, best done in the cool hours with suitable shoes and water. By car you can also reach Vignanotica, which we cover on the HMO page.

Who we recommend it to

Anyone looking for real glamping, with hotel-style services and the feeling of a family-run place: couples, families with children, people travelling with a dog. It works less well for anyone dreaming of absolute silence, for anyone who struggles with high-summer heat, and for anyone without a car.

Prefer olive-grove calm to a resort-style setting? Compare it with Wishne, near Bisceglie.
