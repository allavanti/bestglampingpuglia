Le Lenze Don Mimì: tents among olive trees, a few steps from the sea

Contrada Mattinatella, on the Gargano. A polished glamping site with pool and restaurant that works if you choose the right tent.

Back to home
WhereContrada Mattinatella, Mattinata (FG), Gargano
What it isGlamping among olive trees, steps from the beach
FacilitiesPool, hot tub, restaurant, children’s games
Good to knowYou need a car; the beach is reached by a flight of steps

Le Lenze Don Mimì holds together three things that usually don’t get along: the calm of an olive grove, the comfort of a place where you eat well without getting back in the car, and the sea a step away. The secret is that none of the three overpowers the others.

Among the olive trees of Mattinatella

We are at Mattinatella, a corner of Mattinata overlooking the sea, inside the Gargano National Park. The tents stand among olive trees and are kept with a care you notice straight away: elegant, clean, with air conditioning that on the Gargano in summer is less a luxury than a necessity. The staff are courteous, attentive, always available, and make all the difference on the days when you simply want to think about nothing.

The beach is a few steps away, reached by a flight of steps. On the free section there are umbrellas and sunbeds provided, and on site you can rent kayaks and SUPs to explore the bays and caves along the coast. In the middle of the day the beach can get a little untidy.

The table, and the rest

Food is one of the reasons to stay. Breakfast and the restaurant focus on local produce, and dinner with a sea view is a moment you remember. If you stay a few days, not having to organise every evening is a real comfort. The rest is a little like a hotel: pool, hot tub, fitness area, outdoor games for children, pets allowed. The atmosphere is calm and relaxing, especially suited to couples.

Things to bear in mind

Some tents are small, and after a couple of nights you feel it: aim for the larger ones. In August the heat can make itself felt even with air conditioning, and if you have never slept in a tent, allow for wind and night-time noises. The outdoor tub is surrounded by still-young plants, so privacy is not total. And you hardly get anywhere on foot: for restaurants, shops and trips around the area you need a car.

Nearby

The Baia delle Zagare, with its two sea stacks, is the iconic bay of this stretch of the Gargano, and from the property you reach it by car. In high season access to the free beach may require a permit, with a limited number of daily entries, and parking near the bay is limited and often paid: the rules change every year, so check beforehand. Anyone who prefers to walk has a path from Mattinatella, of medium difficulty and about 90 minutes each way, best done in the cool hours with suitable shoes and water. By car you can also reach Vignanotica, which we cover on the HMO page.

Who we recommend it to

Couples who want glamping with real services, and families looking for something more organised than an agri-camp. It works less well for anyone dreaming of absolute silence, and less still for anyone without a car. In a tent, pick the biggest size you can afford.

Prefer olive-grove calm to a resort-style setting? Compare it with Wishne, near Bisceglie.
