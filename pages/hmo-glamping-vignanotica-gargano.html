HMO at Vignanotica: a tent that feels like a suite

Sleeping in a tent, with the feel of a suite: a private garden, a tub, a generous breakfast and the bay of Vignanotica five minutes on foot.

Back to home
WhereVignanotica, Mattinata (FG), Gargano National Park
What it isSuite-style tents with private garden and tub (this page covers only these)
BeachAbout 450 m, a five-minute walk; electric golf carts for the climb back
Good to knowIsolated, you need a car; no mobile signal, but Wi-Fi

Calling it glamping is a little narrow. HMO has tents, but those who have slept in them describe something else above all: the impression of a very well kept suite that chose canvas over walls. A spacious tent with wood, rugs, air conditioning and a garden of its own, on the hill above the bay of Vignanotica, among the olive trees of the Gargano.

This page covers the tents only. HMO also has rooms, themed suites and a villa, with a different story and a different price: they are not part of this account.

Inside the tent: more cottage than campsite

You step in and it does not feel like a tent. The spaces are wide, the furnishing is careful down to the details, the beds are comfortable, the fridge is a real one (not a hotel minibar) and the climate is always under control: warm and cosy even at the end of October, cool in summer. The bathroom is large, with a roomy shower and, in some tents, a tub you stay in happily for a long time; in others there is an outdoor hot tub, wood-fired. The welcome is a small ritual: fresh fruit, a bottle of sparkling wine, juices and water in the fridge.

What surprises most is outside. Every tent has a private garden among the olive trees, with a table and armchairs: it is where the evening aperitif ends up happening, with the sea at the end of the view. There is no fence around it: anyone travelling with a dog (they are allowed here) should take that into account.

The people, the table, the slow days

Andrea and Lorenzo, and the whole staff behind them, are the reason many guests come back year after year. The welcome is warm without being intrusive, and often informal: the bill, for instance, is settled on departure, without hurry. At the end of the season, even with a single family in the property, the service does not shrink: a dedicated menu, a carefully made dinner, a full breakfast.

Breakfast is a high point: a rich buffet, eggs, warm bread, croissants and house-made pastries. Dinner is served in the olive garden, under the breeze and soft lights, with one menu from the land and one from the sea, local fish and cheeses, generous portions. The choice is short, and some would like it a little longer: but it is the choice of people who want fresh produce and no waste. Lunch can be requested, booked in advance.

The bay, five minutes away

Vignanotica is about 450 metres away, a five-minute walk along a path through the green, with a stretch downhill; anyone who prefers not to walk can be driven in the electric golf carts reserved for guests. On the beach there is an area dedicated to the property, with extra-large padded sunbeds, big umbrellas and a refreshment point. Canoes are available to go and see the caves in the cliff up close.

What to keep an eye on. The climb back from the beach, in the hot hours, is felt: the golf cart exists for this, and it is worth using. In summer, at weekends, the bay fills with boats and day visitors, while the calm of the property stays up on the hill. The menu is short, and anyone looking for plenty of variety at dinner may find it too contained. The price is that of a suite, and not everyone finds it proportionate to a tent, however refined.

Isolated, and that is fine

The position has a price. There is nothing around: you need a car to get here, and water or snacks need to be planned. There is no mobile signal, but the Wi-Fi works well, even on the beach: for many it is the chance to switch off without losing touch with the world. Sauna, sun deck, restaurant, bar and check-in at any hour complete the offer; bicycles can be hired and excursions arranged. If you accept organising yourself a little, the isolation becomes a virtue.

Nearby: Vignanotica, what to expect

The beach is about 700 metres long, a mix of sand, gravel and pebbles at the foot of a tall cliff of white limestone, with caves and recesses that give shade. In summer there are two beach clubs with sunbeds, umbrellas and a refreshment point, but a free section remains; out of season there is no service at all, no bar or kiosks, and you need to bring water and food. Anyone not staying here has to use private car parks and a shuttle, while from HMO you walk there in a few minutes.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

The seabed drops away very quickly, so the bay is less suited to small children and weak swimmers: with the little ones you need armbands and a lot of attention. For older ones there is snorkelling and canoes to the nearby caves. In summer, tourist boats and day visitors arrive in the morning and the bay fills up: to enjoy it more, go early or in the afternoon, when the cliff casts its shade.

Mattinata is about 20 km away, Vieste about 25. In summer there is a bus connection from Mattinata, but timetables change from year to year.

Who it is for

For anyone who wants to sleep in a tent without giving up the comforts of a suite, and the most beautiful Gargano without the crowds. Couples above all, for a birthday or a slow weekend; but also families with children used to walking a little: the sea here drops away quickly, and the little ones need attention. Less suited to anyone seeking nightlife or services within reach, or a true outdoor campsite: this is something else, softer and more private.
