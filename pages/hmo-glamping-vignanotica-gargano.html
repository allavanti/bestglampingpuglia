HMO at Vignanotica: silence, a pebble bay and a path to walk

About 450 metres from one of the best-known bays on the Gargano. A place guests praise almost without reservation, but isolated: it is good to know.

Back to home
WhereVignanotica, Mattinata (FG), Gargano National Park
What it isGlamping tents and rooms/suites
BeachAbout 450 m on foot, with an electric shuttle for the climb back
Good to knowIsolated spot, you need a car

Vignanotica is a bay tucked between the white cliffs of the Gargano, with a pebble beach and clear water. HMO stands a few steps away, and conveys what it means to sleep in a place like this: no noise, no hurry, and the sea a few minutes on foot.

A place that keeps its promise

The first distinguishing trait is who works there. The staff are kind, available, ready to give advice about the area, and leave the impression of people who truly care about the place. The tents and rooms are comfortable, well furnished, with everything you need, and cleanliness is almost impeccable. You feel at ease without effort.

Breakfast and dinner deserve a mention of their own: fresh produce, carefully prepared dishes. In an isolated spot, where you don’t walk to a restaurant, that is real value. And then there is the quiet: peace, silence, nature all around. You need nothing else.

The path to the beach. The bay is reached on foot in a few minutes, about 450 metres, but in the heat the walk can be tiring, especially on the way back. The property provides electric shuttles for the climb. If you have mobility difficulties or very small children, ask how it works before booking.

Isolated, and that is fine

The position has a price. There is no shop around the corner: water and a few snacks need to be planned, and you need a car to get here. Mobile signal is poor, while the Wi-Fi works very well. If you accept organising yourself a little, the isolation becomes an asset.

Sauna, hot tub, sun deck, restaurant, bar, check-in at any hour, organised excursions and high chairs complete the offer. Pets are allowed. On the beach there is a service with sunbeds, umbrellas and a refreshment point.

Nearby: Vignanotica, what to expect

The beach is about 700 metres long, a mix of sand, gravel and pebbles at the foot of a tall cliff of white limestone, with caves and recesses that give shade. In summer there are two beach clubs with sunbeds, umbrellas and a refreshment point, but a free section remains; out of season there is no service at all, no bar or kiosks, and you need to bring water and food. Anyone not staying here has to use private car parks and a shuttle, while from HMO you walk there in a few minutes.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

The seabed drops away very quickly, so the bay is less suited to small children and weak swimmers: with the little ones you need armbands and a lot of attention. For older ones there is snorkelling and canoes to the nearby caves. In summer, tourist boats and day visitors arrive in the morning and the bay fills up: to enjoy it more, go early or in the afternoon, when the cliff casts its shade.

Mattinata is about 20 km away, Vieste about 25. In summer there is a bus connection from Mattinata, but timetables change from year to year.

Who it is for

For anyone who wants the most beautiful Gargano without the crowds and is willing to organise a little: water, car, shoes suited to the path. Couples above all, and those travelling with older children. Less suited to anyone seeking nightlife or services within reach.
